# Card data and artwork

Palvault is an unofficial portfolio project. Palworld and its characters and
card artwork belong to their respective rights holders, including Pocketpair
and Bushiroad. No affiliation or endorsement is claimed. Third-party artwork
is not original portfolio artwork and is not covered by any licence for the
application's source code.

All 256 English printings and their published reference stats were checked
against the [official English card catalogue](https://en.palworld-official-cardgame.com/cardlist/searchresults/?expansion=EBP01)
on 10 September 2026. See [catalogue coverage](CATALOGUE.md) for the complete scope.
Gameplay text and rulings remain on the official site, accessible from the app.
User-created cards have no inferred artwork or gameplay stats.

The bundled card images are unmodified official previews. Every printing's
original image URL and official detail URL are recorded in
[catalogue.json](../palvault/assets/catalogue.json), alongside its official ID,
set, exact printed number, rarity, and parallel status. Images under
`palvault/assets/official/` use official IDs as filenames, avoiding collisions
between Soul cards with the same printed number in different trial decks.

The documented screenshot uses a disposable example collection. Its purchase
price and owned quantities are illustrative, not market prices or private data.
